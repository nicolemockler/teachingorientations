Teaching Orientations Inventory

Adapted from Schiro (2013)

What's your teaching orientation?

This activity identifies your current profile across Schiro's (2013) four teaching orientations. Work through six rounds, each with four cards. In each round, arrange the cards from the view most like yours (1) to least like yours (4).

Drag and drop to rank the cards — or on mobile, tap a card to select it then tap a slot to place it.
